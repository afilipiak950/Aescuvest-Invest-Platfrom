ADVISORY BOARD AGREEMENT

This Advisory Board Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 15, 2025,
between TechVentures AI Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Company"), located at
123 Innovation Drive, San Francisco, CA 94105, and Dr. Michael Chen ("Advisor").

WHEREAS, TechVentures AI Inc. is an artificial intelligence technology company
focusing on enterprise machine learning solutions;

WHEREAS, the Company desires to engage the Advisor to provide strategic guidance
and technical expertise;

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1. ENGAGEMENT
   TechVentures AI Inc. hereby engages Dr. Michael Chen as a member of its
   Advisory Board, effective immediately.

2. SERVICES
   The Advisor shall provide strategic advice and guidance to TechVentures AI Inc.
   regarding technology development, market positioning, and business strategy.

3. COMPENSATION
   The Advisor shall receive 0.25% equity stake in TechVentures AI Inc.

4. TERM
   This Agreement shall continue for a period of two (2) years.

5. COMPANY INFORMATION
   Company: TechVentures AI Inc.
   CEO: Sarah Johnson
   Industry: Artificial Intelligence / Machine Learning
   Founded: 2022
   Headquarters: San Francisco, California
